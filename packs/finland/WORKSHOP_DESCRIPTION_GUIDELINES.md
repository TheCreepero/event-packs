Historical flavor events for Finland, from 1936 to the end of the Winter War in March 1940. Each event is drawn from a sourced moment of the period, from politics and the economy to sport, culture and the defence of the country, and shows a period photograph. The events give small, fitting effects and leave vanilla Finland's own focuses and events alone. Inspired by the old “Third Reich Events” mod made for Hearts of Iron III: Black ICE.

[h1]Info:[/h1]
- Works alone and beside any other Event Pack.
- Events change the game checksum: not Ironman compatible.
- The effects are small, so they shouldn't affect balance too much.
