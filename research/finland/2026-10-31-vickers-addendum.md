Replaces the old section in `2026-10-31-dossier.md`, which misread the delivery dates and the guns. Based on full fetches of the Tank Encyclopedia page and the Jaeger Platoon page (and the English Wikipedia article for cross-checking).

## vickers_six_ton_tanks
Used: 2026-10-31-finland-first-events.md
Checked: 2026-11-01-finland-audit.md, 3 kept, 2 fixed, 0 dropped. The facts below are the researcher's notes, not the checked ones: see source/finland/fact-sheets.md.
Date: order 20 July 1936; first batch arrived July 1938; 26 tanks delivered by 30 November 1939
Kind: military
Suggested window: 1938-07-01 to 1938-09-30 (starts when the first batch arrived), at peace
Facts:
- On 20 July 1936 the Ministry of Defence ordered 32 Vickers-Armstrongs 6-Ton Tank Alternative B, to form a battalion of two 15-tank companies and a 2-tank HQ element. Source: https://tanks-encyclopedia.com/ww2/finland/vickers-6-ton-in-finnish-service/ (Vickers 6-ton in Finnish service); also https://www.jaegerplatoon.net/TANKS2.htm (Armoured Vehicles Part 4: "Finland ordered the 32 Vickers 6 ton tanks Type E Alt B 20th of July 1936.")
- The agreed schedule was 11 tanks by 20 July 1937, 10 by 1 April 1938 and 11 by 1 January 1939. These were agreed dates, not actual arrivals. Source: both pages above.
- The first batch actually arrived in July 1938, delayed by Vickers-Armstrongs' own problems; Tank Encyclopedia gives no actual months for the second and third batches. Jaeger Platoon says sixteen came in 1938, ten more before the Winter War in 1939 and the last six during or after it, and states Vickers "failed to meet this delivery schedule" without giving a cause. Source: both pages above. Month of each later batch: UNVERIFIED.
- The tanks were ordered without optics, radios or armament "to help save money" (Tank Encyclopedia); Jaeger Platoon adds turret rotation mechanisms, tools and in some cases driver's seats. The omissions brought the price to £4,500 per tank (Tank Encyclopedia). Source: both pages above.
- The delivered tanks differed from a standard Mark E: a larger Rolls-Royce engine, a lengthened hull and the turret offset to the right. Source: Tank Encyclopedia page.
- After arrival the tanks went to the State Artillery Factory, Valtion Tykkitehdas (VTT), to be fitted with guns, optics, tools and seats. Equipping was slowed by VTT's slow production, parts sourcing, and the worsening European situation (Tank Encyclopedia); VTT was officially opened only in February 1939, radios were never acquired, and Germany refused to deliver the ordered Zeiss gun sights because of the Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact (Jaeger Platoon). Source: both pages above.
- The gun fitted was the 37 mm Psv.K/36 (L/45), a tank adaptation of the licence-built Bofors anti-tank gun, in a turret mantlet of Bofors design, plus a modified Suomi M/31 submachine gun in a hull port. Vickers' offered 47 mm low-velocity gun was rejected for lacking penetration. Source: Tank Encyclopedia page.
- Guns were fitted at VTT after delivery. "The first armed Vickers arrived from the VTT in mid-December" 1939 (Tank Encyclopedia). Jaeger Platoon gives VTT deliveries of armed tanks: 1 on 14 December 1939, 6 on 6 January 1940, 1 on 12 January 1940 and 10 on 8 February 1940, 18 in total; by the end of December 1939 VTT had made only ten of the 33 main guns ordered. Source: both pages above.
- In the summer 1939 war games, tanks carried only temporary armament (an M/09-31 machine gun and/or a 37 mm Puteaux SA-18 firing blank ammunition) according to Jaeger Platoon; Tank Encyclopedia says the Puteaux was used for training but "this was not the case" for Vickers at the Winter War's outbreak. The two pages differ; trust neither for the exact summer 1939 fit. Source: both pages above.
- Delivered by the start of the Winter War (30 November 1939): 26 of the 32 ("only 26 tanks had been delivered", Tank Encyclopedia); about 6 more arrived from Britain after the war began. Jaeger Platoon: "none of them was combat-ready". Sources disagree with other counts of 33 tanks (including an evaluation tank, Wikipedia). Source: both pages above; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vickers_6-ton (Vickers 6-ton).
- Tank Encyclopedia says by the end of 1939 only 10 tanks were ready; Jaeger Platoon says 18 armed tanks were delivered by 8 February 1940 and issued to 4th Company (16 tanks, 23 February 1940) and 3rd Company (2 tanks). Source: both pages above.
- Wikipedia (not the preferred source here) says the tanks were re-equipped with 37 mm Bofors after the war began, only 13 reached the front in time, and 6 of 13 took part in the first assault at Honkaniemi on 26 February 1940. This conflicts with the VTT fitting timeline above; use only with a second source. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vickers_6-ton (Vickers 6-ton)
- The 1933 test purchase mentioned in the earlier dossier: not confirmed in the fetched pages; UNVERIFIED. Parola Armour Museum exhibit details: UNVERIFIED (not fetched).
