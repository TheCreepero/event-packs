Kind: science/exploration

- First Polish expedition to Greenland, 1937, fieldwork in western Greenland at the eastern edge of Arfersiorfik Fjord. Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/polar-record/article/first-polish-greenland-expedition-1937-on-the-80th-anniversary-of-the-event/3CE03F1E84CCE08940714D98EC872DEE (First Polish Greenland Expedition 1937: on the 80th anniversary of the event, Polar Record).
