Date: 23 April 1937 (law); 11 November 1937 (first state celebration)
Kind: politics
Suggested window: 23 April to 11 November 1937, at peace or not; no war condition needed
Facts:
- The holiday was given the rank of state holiday by a law of 23 April 1937. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narodowe_Święto_Niepodległości (Narodowe Święto Niepodległości). Quote: «Rangę święta państwowego nadano Świętu Niepodległości dopiero ustawą z dnia 23 kwietnia 1937 r.»
- The law is published as Dz. U. 1937 nr 33, poz. 255. Source: same page. Quote: «Dz. U. z 1937 r. Nr 33, poz. 255» (as returned by the fetch; wording around it not copied)
- The holiday was meant to link regained sovereignty with the end of the First World War. Source: same page. Quote: «Miało ono łączyć odzyskanie suwerenności państwowej z zakończeniem I wojny światowej.»
- Earlier celebrations were military ceremonies, usually on the first Sunday after 11 November. Source: same page. Quote: «Organizowano je zazwyczaj w pierwszą niedzielę po 11 listopada.»
- State celebrations before the war took place only twice. Source: same page. Quote: «Do czasu wybuchu II wojny światowej obchody państwowe odbyły się dwa razy: w 1937 r. i 1938 r.»
- In 1937 the celebration included the unveiling of a monument to General Józef Sowiński. Source: same page (paraphrase returned by the fetch; no exact line copied) UNVERIFIED as quote.
- The 23 April 1937 date is also stated by the pl.wikipedia 1937 year page. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937 (1937). Quote: «Sejm ustanowił dzień 11 listopada Świętem Niepodległości.»
- English Wikipedia: Parliament said the day should be "associated with the great name of Józef Piłsudski". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Piłsudski's_cult_of_personality (Józef Piłsudski's cult of personality). Quote: «In 1937 Poland's Parliament declared November 11 a national holiday celebrating the country's restored independence»
Anchors: 23 April 1937; 11 November; Dz. U. nr 33, poz. 255; Sejm; first Sunday after 11 November; General Józef Sowiński
Choice: How to mark the first state holiday: military parade, civic celebrations for all, or tie it to Piłsudski's name.
Picture ideas: Troops marching past a tribune in Warsaw on 11 November; flags and crowds on Plac Saski.

- The crypt was the one under the Silver Bells Tower at Wawel (St. Leonard's crypt held the coffin before). Source: same page (paraphrase of the fetch; no exact line copied) UNVERIFIED as quote. The 1937 year page also names the crypt: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937 (1937). Quote: «przeniesieniu trumny marszałka Piłsudskiego do krypty pod Wieżą Srebrnych Dzwonów»
- The Marshal's committee and President Mościcki opposed it. Source: Konflikt wawelski. Quote: «Tak samo postąpił Naczelny Komitet Uczczenia Pamięci Marszałka Józefa Piłsudskiego oraz prezydent Ignacy Mościcki.»
- Prime Minister Składkowski offered his government's resignation in protest and it was not accepted. Source: Konflikt wawelski. Quote: «demonstracyjnie złożył dymisję swojego rządu, która nie została jednak przyjęta»
- The conflict ended after a Sejm session. Source: Konflikt wawelski. Quote: «Konflikt zakończył się po posiedzeniu Sejmu, na którym oświadczono, że arcybiskup krakowski przeprosił prezydenta.»

- The transfer date of the night of 23/24 June and the 17 June letter to Gen. Wieniawa-Długoszowski come from the fetch's summary of the page, not from a copied line. UNVERIFIED as quote.
Anchors: Adam Sapieha; Wieża Srebrnych Dzwonów; 22 June 1937; Ignacy Mościcki; Felicjan Sławoj Składkowski; Naczelny Komitet Uczczenia Pamięci Marszałka Józefa Piłsudskiego
Choice: Back the president and the Marshal's committee against the archbishop, seek a quiet settlement with the Church, or let the Sejm decide.
Picture ideas: Wawel cathedral and the entrance to the crypts; a crowd outside the cathedral.

## pilsudski_mound_1937
Date: 6 August 1934 to 9 July 1937
Kind: society (commemoration, public works)
Suggested window: July 1937; peace
Facts:
- The mound was begun on 6 August 1934, the 20th anniversary of the First Cadre Company's march from Kraków. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kopiec_Józefa_Piłsudskiego_w_Krakowie (Kopiec Józefa Piłsudskiego w Krakowie). Quote: «Kopiec rozpoczęto sypać 6 sierpnia 1934 r. w dwudziestą rocznicę wymarszu z Krakowa I kompanii kadrowej Legionów.»
- Lieutenant Franciszek Supergan had the idea. Source: same page. Quote: «Pomysłodawcą powstania kopca był por. Franciszek Supergan.»
- A building committee in Warsaw was led by Colonel Walery Sławek. Source: same page. Quote: «W Warszawie powstał Komitet Budowy Kopca na czele którego stanął płk Walery Sławek.»
- Earth from all First World War battlefields was placed in it. Source: same page. Quote: «W kopcu złożono ziemię z wszystkich pól bitewnych I wojny światowej» (the fetch truncated the sentence)
- Piling ended on 9 July 1937. Source: same page. Quote: «Sypanie zakończono 9 lipca 1937 r.»
- Height about 35 m. Source: same page. Quote: «wysokość: ok. 35 m»
- Base diameter 111 m and volume about 130,000 m³ are paraphrases from the fetch, no exact line copied. UNVERIFIED as quote.
- The page does not give a 1937 unveiling ceremony. Do not invent one.
Anchors: Kopiec Piłsudskiego; Sowiniec; Kraków; 6 sierpnia 1934; 9 lipca 1937; ok. 35 m; Franciszek Supergan; Walery Sławek
Choice: Present the mound as a national place of pilgrimage, fund its finishing from the state, or leave it to the committee and volunteers.
Picture ideas: Volunteers carrying soil up the Sowiniec hill; the finished mound over Kraków.

## pilsudski_name_act_1938
Date: passed 7 April 1938, in force 13 April 1938
Kind: politics (law, press)
Suggested window: February to April 1938; peace
Facts:
- The act was passed on 7 April 1938 and the president signed it the same day. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_on_the_protection_of_the_name_of_Józef_Piłsudski (Act on the protection of the name of Józef Piłsudski). Quote: «The act was passed on 7 April 1938» (the fetch returned this as a summary line; confirm against the page)
- Article 2: insulting Piłsudski's name is punished by up to 5 years' imprisonment. Source: same page. Quote: «Whoever insults the Name of JÓZEF PIŁSUDSKI shall be subject to imprisonment for up to 5 years.»
- Article 1 puts his memory under the law's special protection. Source: same page. Quote: «under the special protection of the law»
- The trigger was Stanisław Cywiński's review of Melchior Wańkowicz's book COP – ognisko siły in Dziennik Wileński on 30 January 1938. Source: same page (summary by the fetch, no copied line). UNVERIFIED as quote.
- On 14 February 1938 officers of General Stefan Dąb-Biernacki assaulted Cywiński and other journalists. Source: same page (summary). UNVERIFIED as quote.
- The Senate adopted the act on 23 March 1938; the Sejm was unanimous. Source: same page (summary). UNVERIFIED as quote.
- Prime Minister Składkowski: the act was «the shield on which the entire Polish Nation raises the Name of Józef Piłsudski high.» Source: same page, English rendering of a Polish statement; original Polish not seen. UNVERIFIED as a Polish quotation; do not quote the speaker directly in event text.
- The pl.wikipedia 1938 year page confirms the date. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938 (1938). Quote: «została uchwalona Ustawa o ochronie Imienia Józefa Piłsudskiego»
Anchors: ustawa z 7 kwietnia 1938; art. 2; do 5 lat więzienia; Stanisław Cywiński; Melchior Wańkowicz; Dziennik Wileński
Choice: Back the strict law, accept it with limits for the press, or ask for the penalty to be lowered. Avoid presenting the act as admirable; keep tone neutral.
Picture ideas: Newspaper stands and a courtroom; a Piłsudski bust with flowers.

Date: May 1936 to 1939
Kind: society, economy (city and port development, housing)
Suggested window: 1936-1939; peace
Facts:
- Gdynia had 83,000 inhabitants in May 1936, 102,000 in December 1936 and 127,000 in 1939. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historia_Gdyni (Historia Gdyni). Quote: «W maju 1936 roku miasto liczyło 83 tys. mieszkańców, w grudniu tego samego roku 102 tys., zaś w 1939 roku już 127 tys.»
- The growth was driven by workers coming from all over the country, especially Pomerania. Source: same page. Quote: «Gwałtowny wzrost liczby mieszkańców spowodowany był napływem rąk do pracy z całego prawie kraju, a szczególnie z Pomorza.»
- In the 1930s about 30% of the people lived in around 2,500 primitive huts and shacks. Source: same page. Quote: «Pomimo rozwoju, w latach 30. około 30% ludności mieszkało w ok. 2,5 tys. prymitywnych bud i baraków»
- The port handled 8,700,000 tons in 1938 against 2,923,000 in 1929. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Gdynia (Port of Gdynia). Quote: «1938: 8,700,000 tons» and «1929: 2,923,000 tons»
- Sources differ: a period Polish article seen in search gives 9,311,331.8 tons (year not stated). `[via search summary]` UNVERIFIED. Trust the Wikipedia list for 8.7 million, with caution.
- Jan Kiepura gave a concert on Plac Grunwaldzki five days before the war with proceeds for the Fundusz Obrony Morskiej. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historia_Gdyni (Historia Gdyni). Quote: «Pięć dni przed wybuchem II wojny światowej na placu Grunwaldzkim dał koncert Jan Kiepura» and «a dochód przeznaczono na Fundusz Obrony Morskiej.» That fact is tied to the war date; do not use it for a date-free event.
Anchors: Gdynia; 83 tys. (May 1936); 102 tys. (December 1936); 127 tys. (1939); 2,5 tys. baraków; 8,7 mln ton (1938)
Choice: Spend on new housing and streets for the migrant workers, on more quays, or on both with a loan.
Picture ideas: New modernist apartment blocks next to wooden huts; cranes and quays of the port.
