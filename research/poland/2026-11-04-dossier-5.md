## carol_ii_visit_1937
Date: 26 June to 1 July 1937
Kind: politics
Suggested window: 25 June 1937 to 5 July 1937, at peace.

Facts:
- King Carol II of Romania accepted the honorary chief's post of the 57th Greater Poland Infantry Regiment during his 1937 visit to Poland. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/57_Pułk_Piechoty_Wielkopolskiej (57 Pułk Piechoty Wielkopolskiej). Quote: «objął honorowe szefostwo nad 57 pułkiem piechoty wielkopolskiej»
- On 28 June 1937 President Ignacy Mościcki gave the regiment the name "57 pułk piechoty Karola II Króla Rumunii". Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/57_Pułk_Piechoty_Wielkopolskiej (57 Pułk Piechoty Wielkopolskiej). Quote: «57 pułk piechoty Karola II Króla Rumunii»
- On 28 June 1937 the regimental colours received the Romanian Order of Michael the Brave, 3rd class. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/57_Pułk_Piechoty_Wielkopolskiej (57 Pułk Piechoty Wielkopolskiej). Quote: «Order Michała Walecznego» [tool summary says "3rd class"; confirm the wording on the page]
- Beck visited Romania after his rest in April 1937. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Beck (Józef Beck). Quote: «Po zakończeniu wypoczynku w kwietniu 1937 Beck złożył wizytę w Rumunii.»
- The official visit ran from 26 June to 1 July 1937. UNVERIFIED [via search summary: a military-history information sheet]
- The king arrived at Śniatyń and was greeted by President Mościcki; he and Crown Prince Michael toured the Belweder rooms where Piłsudski had lived; a ball followed at Łazienki. UNVERIFIED [via search summary: 1937 press scans]
- Mościcki had gone to Bucharest with Beck on 6 June 1937 for three days, and Crown Prince Michael came to Warsaw on 24 May with an invitation. UNVERIFIED [via search summary: 1937 yearbook]
- Disagreement: one encyclopaedia page gives 28 April 1937 for the honorary chief's post; the other sources say late June. I trust June. UNVERIFIED [via search summary]

Anchors: Karol II; Mościcki; 57 Pułk Piechoty Wielkopolskiej; Belweder; Biedrusko; 28 czerwca 1937.
Choice: How much ceremony and military honour to give the Romanian ally, and whether to press Romania for firmer commitments against a German or Soviet threat.
Picture ideas: A royal motorcade in Warsaw with honour guards; a regiment parading its colours before two heads of state.

## minorities_declaration_1937
Date: 5 November 1937
Kind: politics
Suggested window: 1 November 1937 to 30 November 1937, at peace with Germany.

Facts:
- The declaration was announced at the same time in Warsaw and Berlin and gave the Polish minority in Germany and the German minority in Poland equal rights. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Beck (Józef Beck). Quote: «dającą mniejszości polskiej w Niemczech i niemieckiej w Polsce równe prawa»
- A 1934 German-Polish declaration, signed in Berlin on 26 January 1934 by Neurath and Lipski, said the two states would not use force against each other and ran for 10 years. Source: https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/documents/2410-german-polish-agreement-to-assure?mode=text (German-Polish agreement to assure "a permanent peace"). Quote: «For the German Government: C. Freiherr von Neurath» [the tool did not return the "10 years" line word for word; confirm on the page]
- The declaration forbade forced assimilation, and let members of the minorities use their language in speech and writing and form associations. UNVERIFIED [via search summary: 1937 press scans]
- A dr Kaczmarek thanked Hitler on behalf of the Union of Poles in Germany. UNVERIFIED [via search summary]
- A 1995 article says it brought no lasting improvement for Poles in Germany; only a gymnasium at Kwidzyn was allowed. UNVERIFIED [via search summary; the PDF would not open]

Anchors: 5 listopada 1937; Związek Polaków w Niemczech; Lipski; Neurath; 26 stycznia 1934.
Choice: Welcome the declaration as a sign of friendship, or treat it as a German move to be tested by watching how Poles in Germany fare.
Picture ideas: A Polish school class in Germany; a Warsaw newspaper stand with the announcement.

## liberia_mission
Checked: 2026-11-04-poland-first-events.md, 8 kept, 1 fixed, 1 dropped. The facts below are the researcher's notes, not the checked ones: see the plan's "Fact verification".
Date: December 1934 to 1938 (mission); window 1936 to 1938
Kind: economy
Suggested window: 1 January 1936 to 31 December 1938, at peace.

Facts:
- The Polish mission to Liberia, 1934-1938, was a series of diplomatic, commercial and scientific initiatives, organised by the Maritime and Colonial League and the Foreign Ministry. Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/historical-journal/article/polish-mission-to-liberia-19341938-constructing-polands-colonial-identity/43B96D7ADDB108E4A9B454E62F6886BF (The Polish Mission to Liberia, 1934-38). Quote: «The Polish mission to Liberia (1934–8) was a series of diplomatic, commercial, and scientific initiatives»
- Unfulfilled economic promises helped end the mission in 1938. Source: same page. Quote: «unfulfilled economic promises, however, contributed to the ultimate termination of the mission in 1938.»
- In December 1934 a group of Polish pioneers boarded the S.S. Poznań. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_and_Colonial_League (Maritime and Colonial League). Quote: «In December 1934, a group of Polish pioneers boarded the vessel S.S. Poznań»
- By 1938 the League was forced to withdraw from Liberia. Source: same page. Quote: «By 1938, the League was forced to withdraw from Liberia.»
- In 1939 the League had around one million members. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_and_Colonial_League (same). Quote: «in 1939 had around one million members.»
- In 1934 the League bought dozens of plantations in Liberia. Source: https://dzieje.pl/aktualnosci/liga-morska-i-kolonialna (liga morska i kolonialna). Quote: «zakupiono kilkadziesiąt plantacji na terenie Liberii»
- The League's two periodicals, "Sprawy Morskie i Kolonialne" and "Morze", sold 140,000 copies. Source: same page. Quote: «w nakładzie 140 tys. egz.»
- Liberia offered to let Poland recruit a "100-tysięcznej armii" in case of war; the venture failed from climate and American pressure. Source: same page. Quote: «100-tysięcznej armii»
- The Brazil settlement Morska Wola was cancelled in 1938. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_and_Colonial_League (same). Quote: «In 1938, the project was cancelled.»
- Cameroons claims and Beck's colonial statements at Geneva (1936). UNVERIFIED [via search summary]. No page read.

Anchors: Liga Morska i Kolonialna; Liberia; S.S. Poznań; grudzień 1934; Morska Wola; 1 milion członków.
Choice: Back the League's colonial ambitions with state money and diplomacy, or cut the expedition loose.
Picture ideas: Pioneers boarding a ship at Gdynia; a League rally with colonial banners.

Source: https://radiokrakow.pl/aktualnosci/80-lat-temu-16-marca-1939-r-polska-znow-zaczela-graniczyc-z-wegrami (same). Quote: not returned word for word; confirm. ## dar_pomorza_horn_1936
Date: 2-3 March 1936
Kind: culture/sport (maritime)
Suggested window: 1 March 1936 to 15 March 1936, at peace.

Facts:
- The training ship Dar Pomorza rounded Cape Horn on 2-3 March 1936. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dar_Pomorza (Dar Pomorza). Quote: not returned word for word; confirm on the page.
- Under Captain Konstanty Maciejewicz-Matyjewicz it sailed round the world from 16 September 1934 to 3 September 1935. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dar_Pomorza (Dar Pomorza). Quote: «symbol przebytych w czasie podróży 39 tys. Mm.»
- On return to Gdynia a 39-metre pennant hung from the mainmast for roughly 39,000 nautical miles. Source: same page. Quote: «symbol przebytych w czasie podróży 39 tys. Mm.»
- It was the first ship of the Polish merchant flag to circumnavigate the globe; 102 school cruises, 13,384 students trained. Source: same page. Quote: «przepłynął pół miliona mil morskich» [for the half-million miles; the other figures need confirming]
- The ship reached Martinique on 29 December 1937. Source: same page. Quote: not returned word for word; confirm.
- Whether Cape Horn 1936 was a separate voyage from the 1934-35 circumnavigation is unclear. UNVERIFIED

Anchors: Dar Pomorza; Gdynia; Cape Horn; 2-3 marca 1936; 39 000 mil morskich.
Choice: Fund more cruises as a showpiece of Polish maritime ambition, or put money elsewhere.
Picture ideas: A full-rigged ship under sail off a rocky cape; cadets on the yards.
