- Zog went into exile in Greece on 7 April. Source: same page. Quote: «riparò in esilio nella vicina Grecia»
- On 12 April the Albanian assembly offered the crown to Victor Emmanuel III. Source: same page. Quote: «offrendo la corona albanese a Vittorio Emanuele III»
- Queen Geraldine and her two-day-old son Leka left for the southern border at 04:00 on 7 April. UNVERIFIED (page states it per the fetch summary, no quote captured)
- Victor Emmanuel III accepted the crown on 15 April at the Quirinale; the Fascist Grand Council approved the union on 13 April. UNVERIFIED (no quote captured)
- Defenders at Durazzo: gendarmes under Abaz Kupi and sergeant Mujo Ulqinaku. UNVERIFIED (no quote captured)
Anchors: Durazzo; 7 April 1939; King Zog; Galeazzo Ciano; 22,000 soldiers; Victor Emmanuel III
Choice: Present the occupation as a protectorate by treaty, as a full union under the Savoy crown, or hold back for fear of Greek and British reaction.
Picture ideas: Italian troops and warships off Durazzo harbour, April 1939; Albanian crown delegation received in Rome.

- Cards were valid two months. Source: same page. Quote: «validità bimestrale»

- Law of 6 May 1940, no. 577, "Norme per il razionamento dei consumi". UNVERIFIED (search summary only)
Anchors: tessera annonaria; 1 February 1940; 0.6 kg coffee; 6 kg sugar; 36,000 tons
Choice: Ration strictly to save foreign currency, rely on autarky substitutes (chicory, roasted grain), or keep imports and risk reserves.

- In 1938 a Lancia Aprilia killed ten spectators, seven children, after which Mussolini stopped authorising races on public roads. Source: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mille_Miglia (Mille Miglia). Quote: «uscì di strada, finì sulla folla, uccise dieci spettatori, di cui sette bambini»
- Crew were Germans Fritz Huschke von Hanstein and Walter Bäumer; von Hanstein was an SS member (avoid in text and picture). The win itself is only shown by the summarised pages above. UNVERIFIED for exact finish time.
Anchors: Brescia, Cremona, Mantova; nine laps; BMW 328; Alfa Romeo 6C 2500; 1938 accident
Choice: Allow the race on a closed triangle despite 1938, back Italian Alfa Romeo entries, or cancel as war nears.
Picture ideas: Cars leaving the line at Brescia at 4 am; a crowd along a Lombard road.
