# Event Packs Wiki

Historical flavor events for Hearts of Iron IV: one Workshop item per country, and one bundle item, Immersive Events Expanded, that holds every country's events.

## Bundle
- Immersive Events Expanded: every pack below in one item. Take the bundle, or single packs, not both.
